# 03 — Roadmap & Resourcing

## Phased roadmap

- **Months 0–9 — MVP:** SALIS as multi-tenant B2B SaaS + consumer app with **Wave 1**
  (garage booking, parts, inspection). First revenue + supply density. Build **shared rails** here.
- **Months 9–24 — Growth:** **Wave 2** (fleet, roadside) + super-app shell + wallet/identity hardening.
- **Months 24+ — Scale:** **Wave 3** (insurance, rentals, ride-share) — gated by licenses; multi-region.

Regulatory/BD runs **in parallel from month 0** because licenses are slow.

## Total headcount & indicative burn (rough KSA planning figures — validate locally)

| Phase | Timeline | Total headcount (eng + non-eng) | Indicative annual burn |
|---|---|---|---|
| MVP | 0–9 mo | ~15–20 | ~$1.5–3M |
| Growth | 9–24 mo | ~35–50 | ~$5–9M |
| Scale | 24 mo+ | ~70–100+ | ~$12–20M+ |

**Non-headcount costs:** payment processing fees, maps/SMS/push usage, cloud (modest early),
**regulatory licenses + legal**, partner revenue shares, and **marketing/CAC** (usually the
largest line in a marketplace). Licensing + CAC are the big numbers, not infra.
